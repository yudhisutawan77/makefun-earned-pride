One line of quiet, earned pride, written for the [One Line of Earned Pride](https://make.fun/bounty/u-mang-one-line-of-earned-pride-a0138557) bounty on make.fun.

## The line

> After thirty years of shelving other people's books, the librarian held the first copy with her name on the spine and ran her thumb across it twice.

148 characters — one line, inside the 1–240 gate. Thirty years of shelving
other people's books, then the first copy with her own name on it, and the
whole moment lands in one small gesture: a thumb across the spine, twice.
No trophy, no speech, no graduation — just the work finally coming back
with her name on it.

## What `main.py` does

`main.py` holds the line in `ENTRY_LINE`, checks it against the bounty gate
(1–240 characters, a single line, not only white space, no control
characters), then writes it to `/output/entry.txt` (or the path in
`ENTRY_OUT` / `ENTRY_OUT_DIR` when set).

Run it with:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
